Before you start

Logging in

  1. Open the Odoo address on your phone, tablet, or the back-office PC.
  2. Sign in with your own account. Sign-offs and stock counts are recorded under whoever is logged in.
  3. You land on Apps Home ("Select an app to get started"), one tile per app you can use. Tap Restaurant Tasks or Inventory.
  4. To come back here, tap the app-grid icon in the top-left corner of any Odoo page.

If a menu described below is missing for you, ask your manager to check your account.

Put it on your home screen. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: browser menu → Add to Home screen. It then opens like an app.

Part A

Your shift checklist

Completing your shift checklist

Step 1 · Open My Tasks

Restaurant TasksMy Tasks

You get a board with four columns, Draft · Active · Completed · Expired. Only Active lists show by default (the "Active" filter chip is on). Tap the card for today's shift.

Nothing on the board? Your shift list has not been created yet, or your login is not linked to your employee record. Ask your manager.

Step 2 · Read the shift list

The list opens as a form. Across the top runs a status bar Draft → Active → Completed, then the shift details: your name, location, shift start and end, the Checkout Policy (see Step 6) and your Completion %. Under the Tasks tab every task is one row.

Step 3 · A simple checkbox task

  1. Do the job.
  2. Tap the green ✓ Done at the end of the row.
  3. The row turns green, the badge says Done, your completion bar moves up.

Step 4 · A task with a checklist or a photo

Tap the task name to open the task screen. It has its own status bar To Do → In Progress → Done and these tabs:

  • Instructions: notes on how to do the job (only shown when there are some).
  • Checklist: one toggle per sub-item. Flip each as you finish; the checklist bar fills. Every toggle must be on before the task can be marked done.
  • Photo Proof: only for Photo Required tasks. Tap the image box, take or pick a photo (the cleaned grill, the fridge thermometer).
  • Comment: optional note for your manager ("Dishwasher leaking again, used the small one").

Then tap Start (optional, shows you are on it) and Mark Done. If something is missing the task stays open and you get a clear message:

Task "Clean fryer" requires a photo. All checklist items must be completed for task "Close bar".

Use the breadcrumb at the top to go back to the shift list.

Step 5 · Sign off your shift

  1. On the shift list, open the Sign-Off tab (it appears once the list is Active).
  2. Draw your signature in the box with your finger and tap Save.
  3. Finish any remaining tasks.
  4. When the last task is done the list moves to Completed by itself. If it still shows Active, tap Sign Off Shift in the header.

Sign-off refuses with a message if the signature is missing or tasks are still open:

Please provide your digital signature before signing off. 2 task(s) still incomplete. Complete all tasks before signing off.

Step 6 · If you are running late

  • Every 10 minutes the system checks deadlines. An overdue task creates a To-Do activity on your shift list (clock icon in the chatter, and the activity bell at the top of Odoo).
  • Overdue tasks are also escalated: after a set delay your manager is notified, then the next person up.
  • Checkout policy. If your checklist is set to Block Checkout, Attendance will not let you clock out until every task is done:Cannot check out: incomplete tasks with "Block Checkout" policy. Complete all required tasks first.With Show Warning you can clock out, but the incomplete score is recorded on your attendance.

Marked something done by accident? Tell your manager: only they can reset a task back to To Do (this also clears its photo and checklist).

Part B

Counting stock

Counting stock on your phone

Step 1 · Open the counting screen

InventoryOperationsAdjustmentsMobile View: Physical Inventory

The screen has a header Physical Inventory with a Refresh button (ignore Apply All, it is for managers and will refuse you); a Search product… box and a Location dropdown; three chips Total · Counted · Differences; one card per product per location, 20 at a time with Load More at the bottom; and a round + button for products that are missing from the list.

Step 2 · Pick the location you are counting

Choose your area in the Location dropdown (Kitchen/Dry Store, Bar/Fridge 1). Count one location at a time. Search matches the product name, not the reference code.

Step 3 · Read a card

Step 4 · Count a product

  1. Tap the count field. The drawer slides up with the product name and location, a big Counted qty display, and Expected and a live Diff beside it.
  2. Type the number. · is the decimal point, ← deletes.
  3. Shortcuts: ✓ Match fills in exactly the expected quantity (use it when the shelf matches). 0.00 means nothing left. Clear starts over.
  4. Tap Confirm Count. The card updates and a toast confirms: Tomatoes — ✓ Match or Tomatoes — +2.00 difference.
  5. Not sure yet? Skip, the ✕, or a tap outside the drawer closes it without saving.

Counts save the moment you confirm. Several people can count different locations at once. Tap Refresh to see what others have entered.

Step 5 · Correct a mistake

Tap the count field again, enter the right number, Confirm Count. The new value replaces the old one. Nothing is final until your manager applies the count.

Step 6 · Add a product that is not listed

  1. Tap the round + button.
  2. Choose the Location.
  3. Type at least two letters of the Product name and pick it from the suggestions.
  4. Enter the Counted Quantity and tap Save. A card appears (or the existing card's count is updated) and the toast says Product added to inventory!

Step 7 · Finish

When every card in your location shows a counted value, tell your manager. Do not press Apply All: without manager rights you only get Access denied. Only stock managers can apply inventory adjustments.

If your counting screen looks different

Some devices use a two-column version of the counting screen (Physical Inventory (Kanban), Inventory Count (Pro), or the tablet app). Same numpad, same rules:

  • Left column To Count, right column Counted. A card moves right once you confirm.
  • A progress bar at the top (14 / 40 counted).
  • A ✓ Match button directly on each card, so a matching shelf is one tap.
  • A coloured stripe and chip on each card: grey Pending, green ✓ Match, blue +2.00 over, red 1.00 short.
  • Tabs All / To Count / Done and a search box; a Location dropdown on the tablet app.
  • Leave the Validate button to your manager.

Questions that come up

I completed everything but the list still says Active.
Tap Sign Off Shift in the header. Your signature must be saved first.
✓ Done says a photo is required.
Open the task, go to Photo Proof, upload the picture, then Mark Done.
I cannot clock out.
Your list uses Block Checkout and still has open tasks. Finish them or ask your manager.
My count disappeared.
Your manager applied the count: on-hand now equals what you counted and the counted field reset. Check On Hand.
The screen will not scroll on my iPhone.
Known issue after an Odoo update. Tell your manager.
Questions about anything on this page? Ask your shift manager.
