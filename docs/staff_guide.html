Before you start

Logging in Staff

  1. Open the Odoo address on your phone, tablet, or the back-office PC.
  2. Sign in with your own account. Sign-offs and stock counts are recorded under whoever is logged in.
  3. You land on Apps Home ("Select an app to get started"), one tile per app you can use. Tap Restaurant Tasks or Inventory.
  4. To come back here, tap the app-grid icon in the top-left corner of any Odoo page.

Put it on your home screen. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: browser menu → Add to Home screen. It then opens like an app.

Part A

Restaurant Tasks

A1 · Completing your shift checklist Staff

Step 1 · Open My Tasks

Restaurant TasksMy Tasks

You get a board with four columns, Draft · Active · Completed · Expired. Only Active lists show by default (the "Active" filter chip is on). Tap the card for today's shift.

Nothing on the board? Either no list has been generated for your shift yet (ask your manager), or your login is not linked to your employee record (ask the admin).

Step 2 · Read the shift list

The list opens as a form. Across the top runs a status bar Draft → Active → Completed, then the shift details: template, planning shift, your name, location, shift start and end, the Checkout Policy (see Step 6) and your Completion %. Under the Tasks tab every task is one row.

ColumnMeaning
Completion TypeCheckbox: just mark it done. Photo Required: you must upload a picture first.
DeadlineWhen it must be done, counted from the shift start (e.g. "60 min after shift start").
Time remainingLive text: 2h 15m left, Overdue 0h 30m, Completed, No deadline.
Checklist %Progress on the task's sub-items, if it has any.
StatusTo Do In Progress Done

Step 3 · A simple checkbox task

  1. Do the job.
  2. Tap the green ✓ Done at the end of the row.
  3. The row turns green, the badge says Done, your completion bar moves up.

Step 4 · A task with a checklist or a photo

Tap the task name to open the task screen. It has its own status bar To Do → In Progress → Done and these tabs:

  • Instructions: the manager's notes on how to do the job (only shown when there are some).
  • Checklist: one toggle per sub-item. Flip each as you finish; the checklist bar fills. Every toggle must be on before the task can be marked done.
  • Photo Proof: only for Photo Required tasks. Tap the image box, take or pick a photo (the cleaned grill, the fridge thermometer).
  • Comment: optional note for your manager ("Dishwasher leaking again, used the small one").

Then tap Start (optional, shows you are on it) and Mark Done. If something is missing the task stays open and you get a clear message:

Task "Clean fryer" requires a photo. All checklist items must be completed for task "Close bar".

Use the breadcrumb at the top to go back to the shift list.

Step 5 · Sign off your shift

  1. On the shift list, open the Sign-Off tab (it appears once the list is Active).
  2. Draw your signature in the box with your finger and tap Save.
  3. Finish any remaining tasks.
  4. When the last task is done the list moves to Completed by itself. If it still shows Active, tap Sign Off Shift in the header.

Sign-off refuses with a message if the signature is missing or tasks are still open:

Please provide your digital signature before signing off. 2 task(s) still incomplete. Complete all tasks before signing off.

Step 6 · If you are running late

  • Every 10 minutes the system checks deadlines. An overdue task creates a To-Do activity on your shift list (clock icon in the chatter, and the activity bell at the top of Odoo).
  • Overdue tasks can be escalated: after a set delay an email and activity go to your manager, then to the next level (see A3).
  • Checkout policy. If the checklist is set to Block Checkout, Attendance will not let you clock out until every task is done:Cannot check out: incomplete tasks with "Block Checkout" policy. Complete all required tasks first.With Show Warning you can clock out, but the incomplete score is recorded on your attendance.

Marked something done by accident? Only a manager can Reset a task back to To Do (this also clears its photo and checklist). Tell them.

A2 · Setting up and running shifts Manager

Step 1 · Build a checklist template (once)

Restaurant TasksConfigurationTask TemplatesNew
  1. Name it: Opening Shift Checklist, Closing – Kitchen, Weekly Deep Clean.
  2. Location: pick your restaurant to restrict the template to it, or leave empty for everywhere.
  3. Checkout Policy: Show Warning (default) or Block Checkout (staff cannot clock out until all tasks are done). Use Block only for lists staff can always finish.
  4. Optional Description for your own notes.
  5. In the Tasks table tap Add a line per task: drag the handle to reorder, choose Checkbox or Photo Required, tick Has deadline and set the minutes after shift start (30 = due half an hour into the shift). The Checklist column shows how many sub-items the task has.
  6. Save. The chatter records who changed what. Untick Active to retire a template without losing history.

Checklist sub-items (the toggles staff see under a task) are not editable from this table. The admin sets them up (see A3).

Step 2 · Assign a shift list

Restaurant TasksTask ListsNew
  1. Pick the Template, the Employee and the Location.
  2. Set Shift Start and Shift End. Deadlines are calculated from Shift Start, so get it right.
  3. Optionally link the Planning Shift from the Planning app.
  4. Save, then tap Generate Tasks in the header.

Generate Tasks copies every task and sub-item from the template, calculates the deadlines and sets the list to Active. It now shows on the employee's My Tasks board, named Employee — Template — date. Create one list per employee per shift.

Step 3 · Monitor during the shift

  • Task Lists (list, board or form) shows every list for your location: employee, template, shift start, done/total, progress bar, status. Filters: Active, Completed, Expired, Today, This Week. Group by Employee, Location, Status, Shift Date.
  • All Tasks lists every individual task with Overdue / To Do / Done filters. Tap ✓ Done to close a task on someone's behalf, or open it to Reset.
  • Dashboard → Today's Overview: bar chart of today's tasks per employee split by status. Weekly Trends: average completion % per day since Monday. Overdue Tasks: everything currently past its deadline.

Step 4 · Fix problems

SituationWhat to do
Task marked done by mistakeOpen the task → Reset. Photo and checklist are cleared.
Wrong employee or template on a listAn Admin taps Reset on the list (back to Draft, all tasks removed), edits it, then Generate Tasks again.
Staff cannot clock outComplete or reset the open tasks; or switch the template to Show Warning for next time.
Employee sees nothing in My TasksTheir user is not linked to the employee (Employees → employee → HR Settings → Related User).

Step 5 · Where the scores show up

  • Attendance record: a Task Completion section with the completion % and whether checkout is blocked. The attendance list has an optional Task Completion % column.
  • Employee form: an Avg Completion smart button opens all their lists.
  • Planning shift: a Task Lists smart button once lists are linked.

A3 · Rules, access and maintenance Admin

Escalation rules

Example chain: Level 1 at +0 min → assigned employee · Level 2 at +30 min → employee's manager · Level 3 at +90 min → the owner. The job runs every 5 minutes; each level fires once per task.

Access rights

SettingsUsersuserRestaurant Tasks
  • Staff see only lists whose employee's Related User is them, so every staff account must be linked to its employee record.
  • Managers see only lists whose location matches the Work Location on their own employee record. A manager without a work location sees an empty Task Lists screen.
  • Owner / Admin sees everything.

Checklist sub-items on templates

Sub-items live on the individual task template; the Task Templates screen only shows their count. To edit them, activate developer mode (Settings → Activate the developer mode), open the task template record and add lines under Checklist Items. Ask the developer to add an open-form button to the tasks table if you do this often.

Scheduled jobs and email

Two scheduled actions run on their own (Settings → Technical → Scheduled Actions): Restaurant Tasks: Check Overdue every 10 min and Restaurant Tasks: Escalation Chain every 5 min. Outgoing mail must be configured for escalation emails to leave the server.

Part B

Inventory Count

B1 · Counting stock on your phone Staff

Step 1 · Open the counting screen

InventoryOperationsAdjustmentsMobile View: Physical Inventory

The screen has a header Physical Inventory with Refresh and Apply All (managers only, see B2); a Search product… box and a Location dropdown; three chips Total · Counted · Differences; one card per product per location, 20 at a time with Load More at the bottom; and a round + button for products that are missing from the list.

Step 2 · Pick the location you are counting

Choose your area in the Location dropdown (Kitchen/Dry Store, Bar/Fridge 1). Count one location at a time: the list stays short and Apply All stays safe. Search matches the product name, not the reference code.

Step 3 · Read a card

Step 4 · Count a product

  1. Tap the count field. The drawer slides up with the product name and location, a big Counted qty display, and Expected and a live Diff beside it.
  2. Type the number. · is the decimal point, ← deletes.
  3. Shortcuts: ✓ Match fills in exactly the expected quantity (use it when the shelf matches). 0.00 means nothing left. Clear starts over.
  4. Tap Confirm Count. The card updates and a toast confirms: Tomatoes — ✓ Match or Tomatoes — +2.00 difference.
  5. Not sure yet? Skip, the ✕, or a tap outside the drawer closes it without saving.

Counts save the moment you confirm. Several people can count different locations at once. Tap Refresh to see what others have entered.

Step 5 · Correct a mistake

Tap the count field again, enter the right number, Confirm Count. The new value replaces the old one. Nothing is final until a manager applies the adjustment.

Step 6 · Add a product that is not listed

  1. Tap the round + button.
  2. Choose the Location.
  3. Type at least two letters of the Product name and pick it from the suggestions.
  4. Enter the Counted Quantity and tap Save. A card appears (or the existing card's count is updated) and the toast says Product added to inventory!

Step 7 · Finish

When every card in your location shows a counted value, tell your manager. Do not press Apply All: without manager rights you only get Access denied. Only stock managers can apply inventory adjustments.

B2 · Reviewing and applying the count Manager

  1. Open the same screen and select the Location that was counted.
  2. Scan the cards. The Differences chip says how many loaded cards disagree with the system; red-tinted cards are shortages, green-tinted are surpluses. Tap Load More until everything is loaded.
  3. Investigate anything surprising before applying: recount, check the bin next door, check for a pending transfer.
  4. Tap Apply All. A yellow banner asks This will apply all inventory adjustments and create stock moves. Are you sure? Tap Yes.
  5. The toast Inventory adjustments applied! confirms. Odoo posts a stock move for every difference; on-hand now equals the counts, counted values reset, and the cards reload.

Apply All follows the Location dropdown. With All Locations selected it applies every internal location in the warehouse at once. Always select the location first.

Applying cannot be undone from this screen. The moves it creates are listed under Inventory → Reporting → Moves History; a wrong count is fixed by counting again and applying again. Apply All needs the Inventory / Administrator access right.

The same counts also appear in the standard Inventory → Operations → Physical Inventory list, where you can review them with Odoo's usual filters and history.

B3 · If your counting screen looks different Staff

Three other versions of the counting screen exist (Physical Inventory (Kanban) and Inventory Count (Pro) under Inventory → Operations, and a standalone tablet app at /inventory-count). Same numpad, same rules, two columns instead of one list:

  • Left column To Count, right column Counted. A card moves right once you confirm.
  • A progress bar at the top (14 / 40 counted).
  • A ✓ Match button directly on each card, so a matching shelf is one tap.
  • A coloured stripe and chip on each card: grey Pending, green ✓ Match, blue +2.00 over, red 1.00 short.
  • Tabs All / To Count / Done and a search box (Pro and standalone); a Location dropdown (standalone only).
  • Validate in the header is the manager's Apply All. The standalone app asks Validate inventory and apply all adjustments? and applies every counted product in every location, so finish all locations before validating there.

Questions that come up

I completed everything but the list still says Active.
Tap Sign Off Shift in the header. Your signature must be saved first.
✓ Done says a photo is required.
Open the task, go to Photo Proof, upload the picture, then Mark Done.
I cannot clock out.
One of your lists uses Block Checkout and still has open tasks. Finish them or ask a manager to reset them.
My count disappeared.
A manager applied the adjustment: on-hand now equals your count and the counted field reset. Check On Hand.
The screen will not scroll on my iPhone.
Known Odoo issue after an update. Tell the admin (see Maintenance).

Maintenance Admin

After every Odoo 19 update re-apply the iOS scroll fix on the server (commands in the repository README): patch webclient_layout.scss, clear the web.assets attachments, restart the Odoo service. Without it the mobile screens do not scroll on iPhone and iPad.

The WAJ BoM Mobile Fix module only tidies Manufacturing → Bills of Materials on phones (Reference, Type, Company and Unit columns hidden by default; the column picker brings them back). It has no workflow of its own.
